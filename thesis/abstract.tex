\chapter*{Abstract}
\label{abstract}
    \paragraph{}
    This thesis unfolds the conception and realisation of an ontology-driven, knowledge-graph-empowered systems engineering framework, interweaving the rigour of structured semantic models with the nuanced inferential capabilities of large language models, all within the ambit of Sunswift Racing’s trailblazing endeavour to craft the SR8, the world’s first road-legal, student-built tri-brid electric vehicle. Through meticulous formalisation of the Australian Design Rules into a machine-readable ontology, alongside the harmonisation of extensive, heterogeneous engineering artefacts, this research employs language models not only to extract and align complex regulatory requirements but also to propagate risk and verify compliance with a finesse that transcends traditional methodologies. The methodology, a carefully choreographed synthesis of disciplined knowledge extraction and inspired knowledge extension, orchestrates a multi-agent ensemble incorporating neural link prediction and reinforcement learning, ultimately shaping a living knowledge graph that supports interactive, explainable, and contextually rich decision-making. By transforming a fragmented and sprawling corpus of technical and regulatory information into a coherent and queryable tapestry, this work offers a blueprint for elevating regulatory-aligned systems engineering to new heights of scale, adaptiveness, and insight in the evolving realm of advanced automotive design.
